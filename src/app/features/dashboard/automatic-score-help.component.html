Automatic wins and losses

Track your performance as you play, without updating the overlay score after every game.

When Match Vision receives your finished game's result, it adds one win or loss to your session score. Each game counts only once, even if you reopen the app.

Not counted

  • Games against the computer or with any AI participants.
  • Replays and games you observe instead of playing.
  • W3Champions losses in games lasting two minutes or less. Short wins still count.
  • Games without a confirmed result or enough player data to check these rules.

A session score, not a daily total

The score resets after more than five hours without a score update—not at midnight. Counted games and manual score changes keep the session active.

You stay in control

Use + / − to correct the score, or Reset to start at zero. Turning automatic updates off leaves the score available for manual editing; results received while it is off are not added later.
